# Privacy policy

*Annotate, for macOS. Effective October 1, 2026.*

Annotate does not collect, store, sell, or share any information about you. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind, and its developer receives nothing from your use of it.

## Your documents

PDFs you open stay on your Mac. Annotate reads and changes only the files you open or save with it, and keeps your markers, notes, and edits inside those files. It runs in the macOS App Sandbox, which limits it to the files you choose.

## The document assistant

The assistant is optional and does nothing until you use it.

- **Ollama** and **Apple Intelligence** run on your Mac. With them, document text never leaves your Mac.
- **OpenAI API** and **Claude API** are cloud services you choose and pay for with your own API key. Annotate first shows you exactly which passages and request details would be sent, and sends them only when you choose **Send**. The request goes directly from your Mac to that provider and is governed by its privacy policy ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy), [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy)). Annotate asks OpenAI not to store responses.

Your API keys are stored in your Mac's Keychain and are sent only to the provider they belong to. They are never written into documents or sent anywhere else.

## Links

When you click a web or email link in a PDF, Annotate shows you the address and opens it only if you agree. The website or mail app you open has its own privacy practices.

## Children

Annotate does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date. For questions, open an issue at [github.com/mweingartner/annotate/issues](https://github.com/mweingartner/annotate/issues).
