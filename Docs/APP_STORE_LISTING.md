## Description

Annotate is a calm, native PDF workspace for reading closely and editing confidently.

READ AND MARK
• Mark passages as Important, Revisit, Question, or Note, with your own colors, icons, notes, and questions.
• Glass pins sit beside the passages they belong to; the sidebar takes you straight back to each one.
• Export a sharing copy with permanent highlights and an index of your notes and questions.

EDIT IN PLACE
• Click a paragraph and type. Your edit is set like the original: the same font (or the closest installed match), baselines, margins, alignment, and line breaks.
• When a paragraph gains or loses a line, only the content below it moves, by exactly that line.
• Edits are saved as real, selectable PDF text.

A COMPLETE PDF WORKSPACE
• Organize pages: reorder, insert, rotate, split, merge, and extract.
• Create and fill forms; add visible signatures or sign a copy with a certificate, and validate signed files.
• Convert to Word, RTF, text, HTML, Excel, PowerPoint, and images; compress; make scans searchable with OCR.
• Export redacted copies whose removed content can't be recovered.

ASK YOUR DOCUMENT
• Ask questions, summarize, find key details, explain, or translate, with answers linked to the pages and passages they used.
• Use Apple Intelligence or Ollama on your Mac, or your own OpenAI or Claude API key. Cloud requests are shown to you before anything is sent.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking, no data collected.
• Your documents stay on your Mac. Links in PDFs ask before they open.

## Review notes

Annotate needs no account. To try the main features, open any PDF (File › Open Reading Guide opens a sample), select text to add a marker, or choose Edit and click a paragraph to edit it. The assistant works without any setup through Apple Intelligence where available, and with Ollama if it is installed; OpenAI and Claude require the user's own API key and show the exact request before sending.
